# CurbSync

Leave at the perfect time for the airport.

CurbSync combines live flight status with real-time drive times to tell you exactly when to leave — whether you're **catching a flight yourself** or **picking someone up at the curb**.

## Two modes, one tap

When you pick a flight, CurbSync asks how you're using it:

- **Pickup mode** — we wait for the flight to land, factor in deplaning (and baggage claim for Pro), then tell you when to leave so you're at the curb the moment your passenger walks out.
- **Departure mode** — we work backward from your scheduled departure, factor in your preferred airport-arrival buffer (default 2 hours domestic / 3 hours international), and tell you when to leave so you make boarding without the panic-jog.

Switch modes any time without re-picking the flight.

## Why CurbSync

- Live LEAVE IN countdown driven by current traffic and the latest airline data
- Uses your device location by default — no fiddling with addresses
- Departure-mode delay alerts if your flight slips by 30+ minutes
- Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown (Pro)
- Push notifications when it's time to go (Pro)
