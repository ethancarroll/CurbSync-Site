# CurbSync

Leave at the perfect time for airport pickup.

CurbSync combines live flight status and real-time traffic to tell you exactly when to leave for curbside pickup.

## Why CurbSync

- Live LEAVE IN countdown based on flight ETA and current traffic
- Compare multiple pickup spots and choose the fastest route
- Lock Screen and Dynamic Island updates (Pro)
- Push notifications when it is time to go (Pro)

CurbSync is currently available through TestFlight.
